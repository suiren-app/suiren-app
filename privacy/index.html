Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Suiren collects no data. There is no account and no server. Nothing you enter in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

Your habits, check-ins, notes, daily moods (“How you feel”), koi and pond are stored only on your device. The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read the same data from a shared space on your iPhone that only Suiren and its widgets can open.

No analytics, ads or tracking

Suiren contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools and no third-party SDKs. We do not build profiles, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

Suiren+ and decoration sets are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. Apple tells the app only which products you own, so it can unlock them.

Backups

Backups you export, and the optional daily backup the app saves to your own iCloud Drive, belong to you. They are stored under your Apple ID and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We cannot see or open them.

Photos

If you save a picture of your pond, Suiren asks for permission to add it to your photo library. It only adds pictures and never reads your existing photos.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They contain technical details about the crash and none of your habit data.

Children

Suiren is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If Suiren ever starts collecting any data, we will say so here and in the app before it happens.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email suiren-app@gmail.com or see the Support page.
