Version 1.5

Coming soon

Coming to the App Store soon. These notes cover 1.4 and 1.5 together, since 1.4 never reached the App Store.

Music for meditation, a zen garden to rake, a gentler Peaceful mode, and a lot of small kindnesses.

  • Meditation music. Songs by Griffin Hauben now drift through meditation. Tap Music to hear them now and then, all the time, or not at all, with a volume of their own.
  • Rake your gravel. Tap the raked gravel bed from the zen garden set and kneel over it: draw your own pattern to the soft hush of the rake, move the stone wherever you like, and share your design as a card.
  • Peaceful mode. Just want to track? Choose Peaceful for any koi: missed days never cost anything, and everything earns half the XP. Every koi now wears its mode's mark, a leaf, a drop or a flame.
  • Yesterday still counts. Forgot to check in? Log yesterday today. Apple Health also fills in a day you didn't open Suiren.
  • Undo a check-in. Tapped the wrong answer? Undo it, switch it, or fix an amount any time that day from the habit's menu. The XP goes back too.
  • Protein and calories from Apple Health. Link a habit to what your food app logs: reach a protein goal, or stay under a calorie limit.
  • Morning, afternoon and evening. Give a habit a time of day, and your check-in list shows what fits now first.
  • The pond sitter. While you're away, a little tanuki in a straw hat sits by the pond with a cup of tea, and the koi it's minding doze under their own lily pads.
  • Your koi in the Dynamic Island. During a focus session, your koi swims beside the time in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen, and turns into an open lotus when the time is up.
  • A first gift. Your first koi learns to greet you when you open the app, free, and it can never be lost.
  • A gentler start. A new pond's first egg hatches before the tour, and the tour now comes a little at a time, where it matters. The new Guide in the pond menu has all of it, any time.
  • Ask Siri. “Meditate with Suiren” opens the quiet pond, and “Start a focus with Suiren” opens the focus timer.
  • The garden grows. Trees spread a wide canopy, and a Flatten button in build mode reaches anything underneath. Tall grass bows in the wind, wild grass grows around your land, and you can sell a piece you bought for everything you paid.
  • A closer pond. Storybook-sized koi, a pond that reopens where you left it, softer lantern-lit nights, and a Lights tile in meditation for a dark room.
  • Call a koi for a photo. In photo mode, tap the water and the nearest koi swims over and stays a while.
  • Story cards. Pond cards come in a tall 9:16 shape for stories, and a finished friendship pagoda gets a card of its own.
  • Rest days in your widgets. A resting koi rests in your widgets too, and “done” counts only what's due today.
  • VoiceOver in the pond. Every koi and egg is its own spot on the water. Swipe from fish to fish and the camera follows.
  • Smoother and lighter. Feeding a big pond no longer stutters, the pond rests while Settings or the Journal covers it, and the water sounds crisper.
  • Fixes. Habit names show in full in your check-in list, build mode's buttons are clearer, “stronger together” counts only the days a habit is due, a set bought from the shop shows up straight away, the focus timer counts minutes and seconds, and the − and + beside an amount stay in line.

Thank you for keeping your koi company.

Version 1.3

On the App Store

November 26, 2026. The first release.

The first release. Thank you for raising a koi with me.

  • Koi and habits. One koi per habit (3 free, up to 15 with Suiren+). Name an egg, watch it hatch, and raise it from fry to elder, with skills to learn along the way.
  • Your rhythm. Set the days a habit is due, or let the pond sitter mind things while you're away. Normal and Hardcore modes for how strict you'd like it.
  • The garden. Build with the XP you earn: bridges, lanterns, trees and decoration sets, with day and night that follow your clock, and fireflies after dark.
  • Journey and journal. A calendar of the days you kept, milestones and streak gifts, written entries, and a poet's cushion for haikus.
  • How you feel. A daily pond-weather check-in with a monthly heatmap and a 12-week trend.
  • Meditation and focus. A calm meditation mode with haikus, and a focus timer with a Live Activity.
  • Apple Health. Steps, workouts, sleep and mindful minutes can check a habit in for you.
  • Friends. Invite a friend to build a friendship pagoda together, and share pond cards.
  • Widgets and Siri. Home and Lock Screen widgets, a koi carousel, a Meditate widget and control, and “Check in to Suiren”.
  • And more. iCloud Drive backup with export and import, photo mode, an app tour, and Suiren+ with one-time decoration sets.

Already have it? Updates arrive on their own when automatic updates are on, in Settings, App Store.
